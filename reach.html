ARC-001

THE REACH

STATUS: DOCUMENTED

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

NATURE: UNKNOWN

LOCATION OVERVIEW

The Reach is commonly described as another dimension. This classification remains unconfirmed.

Current records describe The Reach as a network of impossible spaces connected to what is believed to be a larger environment known as the Reach World.

ACCESS

Entry into The Reach appears to occur when an individual experiences a discontinuity in reality.

These events are commonly described as "glitches." A sufficiently unstable glitch may develop into a threshold, allowing passage into The Reach.

ENVIRONMENT

The Reach does not appear to contain a conventional landscape.

Instead, it is primarily characterized by a seemingly endless void containing doors and other points of passage. These openings appear to connect to various impossible or glitched locations.

The purpose of these connections is unknown.

KNOWN LOCATIONS

THE HOLLOW

The only consistently documented location currently associated with The Reach.

Further information is contained within separate archival records.

ENTITIES

The Reach is inhabited by numerous entities. Their origins, behavior, and relationship to the environment remain unknown.

Most documented entities are considered dangerous.

OBSERVATION

No reliable method of predicting changes within The Reach has been established.

Locations may change between observations. Objects may appear or disappear. A space may be altered between the time an individual enters it and the moment they look at it again.

ARCHIVAL NOTE

No two records of The Reach should be assumed to describe the same environment.
