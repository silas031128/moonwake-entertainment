THE PLACES THAT SHOULDN'T EXIST

The Places That Shouldn't Exist is a psychological horror novel following Silas as he discovers a town that does not seem to follow the rules of reality.

What begins as an ordinary town slowly becomes something much stranger as familiar locations change, people behave differently, and places appear where they shouldn't exist.
