THE PLACES THAT SHOULDN'T EXIST

SEPTEMBER 2026 LIBRARY

The Places That Shouldn't Exist has been completed and added to the Moonwake Library.

The story follows Silas as he discovers that the town around him is not behaving the way it should.

What begins as an ordinary place slowly becomes something much stranger, with locations changing, familiar places appearing where they shouldn't, and the rules of reality becoming increasingly unreliable.

The book is now part of the official Moonwake Library and represents one of the first completed Moonwake story projects.
