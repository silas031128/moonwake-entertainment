WHAT IS MOONWAKE?

Moonwake Entertainment is an independent creative project focused on games, stories, music, experiments, and other creative projects.

Moonwake was created by Gadiel Guzman in 2026 as a place to bring different creative ideas together and turn them into actual projects.

CURRENT WORK

WHAT WE CREATE

Moonwake currently includes projects such as Northreach: The Atlas, The Places That Shouldn't Exist, Northreach Engine, Moonwake Music, and other experiments.

Projects may take the form of games, books, music, software, hardware experiments, videos, or other creative works.

FUTURE STATUS

THE FUTURE OF MOONWAKE

Moonwake Entertainment is currently a future studio project and has not been officially established as a company or studio.

The goal is to eventually grow Moonwake into a larger creative studio with games, stories, music, and other projects.
