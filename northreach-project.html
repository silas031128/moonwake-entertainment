NORTHREACH: THE ATLAS

Northreach: The Atlas is an exploration game centered around strange worlds, unexplained places, puzzles, horror, fantasy, and science fiction.

The project focuses on exploration and discovery, with locations that may not always follow the rules of the world around them.

PROJECT FEATURES

  • Exploration
  • Horror
  • Puzzles
  • Fantasy
  • Science Fiction
  • Strange and unexplained locations

NORTHREACH ENGINE

Northreach is being developed alongside a custom game engine created specifically for the project.
