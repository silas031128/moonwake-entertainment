ABOUT

Northreach is a 3D exploration and survival game built around Moonwake Entertainment's concept of Reality Expansion.

In Northreach, the world isn't simply a fixed map waiting to be completed. It grows, changes, and reveals new possibilities as you explore it.

You begin in a small, peaceful environment. As you venture farther, you'll discover strange locations, creatures, resources, structures, and unexplained phenomena, each revealing more of a world that seems far larger than it first appeared.

Build. Explore. Survive. Discover.

Northreach isn't about simply defeating enemies or completing a list of objectives. It's about the feeling of stepping into a world that keeps expanding beyond what you thought was possible.

REALITY EXPANSION

Reality Expansion is Moonwake Entertainment's approach to creating worlds that continue to reveal themselves through exploration.

Instead of treating the world as a finished space, Northreach is designed around discovery. The farther you explore, the more possibilities the world can reveal.

DEVELOPMENT

Northreach is currently in development by Moonwake Entertainment.

Development is focused on building the foundations of the world, exploration systems, survival mechanics, and the systems that make Reality Expansion possible.
