ABOUT NORTHREACH

EXPLORE A WORLD THAT CONTINUES TO EXPAND BEYOND WHAT YOU THOUGHT WAS POSSIBLE.

Northreach is a 3D exploration and survival game built around Moonwake Entertainment's concept of Reality Expansion.

Begin in a small, peaceful environment and venture beyond it to discover strange locations, creatures, resources, structures, and unexplained phenomena.

CORE EXPERIENCE

WHAT YOU'LL DO

EXPLORE Travel beyond the familiar and discover places hidden throughout the world.
SURVIVE Adapt to an environment that doesn't always reveal everything at once.
BUILD Create and shape your surroundings as you progress through the world.
DISCOVER Find the unexplained and uncover more of what Northreach has to offer.
