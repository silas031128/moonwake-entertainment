SMART POT

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smart Pot is an experimental hardware project designed to make taking care of plants easier. The project explores how technology can assist with everyday plant care while keeping the system simple and practical.

The long-term concept is to create a plant pot that can monitor its environment and help respond to the needs of the plant.

CORE CONCEPT

Smart Pot is built around the idea of combining a normal plant pot with sensors and automated systems. Instead of relying entirely on manual plant care, the system could monitor important conditions and provide useful information or assistance when needed.

PROJECT GOALS

  • Make plant care easier
  • Monitor plant conditions
  • Experiment with hardware and sensors
  • Explore automated plant-care systems
  • Develop a practical and reusable design

PLANNED FEATURES

  • Soil moisture monitoring
  • Environmental condition monitoring
  • Automated watering
  • Plant-care status feedback
  • Future hardware expansion

CURRENT STATUS

Smart Pot is currently an experimental project. The design and feature set are still being explored, and additional hardware details will be documented as development continues.
