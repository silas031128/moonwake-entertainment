GREYWICK

STATUS: DOCUMENTED

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

LOCATION OVERVIEW

Greywick is a glitched neighborhood containing a connection to The Reach. The exact location of Greywick and the method of reaching it remain unknown.

The neighborhood appears ordinary during initial observation. However, its surroundings may change when they are not being directly observed.

ENVIRONMENT

Greywick appears to resemble an ordinary residential neighborhood. Streets, buildings, signs, and other familiar locations may appear normal at first.

Objects and locations have been observed to change position without explanation. Looking away and looking back may reveal something different from what was previously observed.

RESIDENTS

Residents of Greywick appear human and behave as though they belong in the neighborhood.

Archived observations indicate that these residents are actually entities that have glitched from The Reach into the real world.

Their behavior should not be considered trustworthy.

KNOWN THRESHOLD

Greywick contains multiple documented glitches. One of the most consistent anomalies is an abandoned building located somewhere within the neighborhood.

When an individual reaches the sixth floor of the building, a door may appear behind them.

The door is considered a confirmed threshold connected to The Reach.

Destination beyond the threshold: UNKNOWN.
